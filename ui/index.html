    Station Announcement Maker
    
      
    Exclusively built for MSTS / TSRE5 / Open Rails
    
      
    Part of UTILITY TOOLS
    
      
    Turn any recording into a ready-to-place station announcement: MSTS-safe 16-bit PCM WAV, a matching .sms and a correct ssource.dat entry — written straight into your route.
    
      
    
        
    Instant conversionMP3, M4A, OGG, FLAC and more to 22 050 / 44 100 Hz 16-bit PCM.
    
        
    Direct route integrationWrites into the route's SOUND folder and appends ssource.dat in its own UTF-16 encoding.
    
        
    Batch generationA whole line's worth of stations in one go.
    
        
    Announcement managerBrowse, play and swap any route sound without touching .sms or ssource.dat.
    
        
    Safe by defaultssource.dat.bak backups, overwrite prompts and kept copies of replaced audio.
    
        
    Format checksFlags MP3-in-WAV, 24-bit and other files that MSTS / OR can't play.
