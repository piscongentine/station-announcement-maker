Getting started

Tip: the FAQ tab above answers 66 common questions — search it first.

1. Open Settings and pick your MSTS / Open Rails folder (the one with ROUTES). 2. Make announcements in Single or Batch. 3. Place them on the route in 3D Sound Placement, listen, and Save.

Make announcements (Single / Batch)

Drop an audio file, name it, and choose Smart Direct Route Integration. The app writes Name.wav (16-bit PCM) and Name.sms into the route's SOUND folder and adds a line to ssource.dat (a copy is kept as ssource.dat.bak). The new sound then appears in the Sound to place list of the 3D editor and in TSRE5.

Same name already there? You are asked before anything is overwritten. In a batch, repeated names get _2, _3. A name already in ssource.dat is never added twice.

Route Announcement Manager

Shows every ssource.dat entry with playback and format checks. Replace Audio swaps only the .wav; the old file is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\StationAnnouncementMaker\replaced.

3D Sound Placement

Pick a route and press Load. You see the real terrain (from the route's TILES), the track (from the .tdb), every station and its platforms — drawn as boxes shaded at 30 % with a border, labelled like the Track Viewer (NAIHATI JUNCTION PF1 · PLATFORM); click a platform label to pick its station — and every placed sound (from the WORLD\*.ws files) — the same data TSRE5 and Open Rails use. Look around 360° by dragging with the mouse. Map view (M) looks straight down for quick travel.

Place, select, remove

  • Place: click the ground — a cyan pointer node appears with its position, tile, ground height and the nearest stations. Pick a sound from the Sound to place list (these are the route's ssource.dat entries) and press Place sound at pointer. New sounds are green until saved.
  • Select: click a sound in the view, or click its name in the Sounds list. The selection turns amber (marker, label and list bar) with a pulsing amber ring and its rings are drawn on the ground: activation distance, where it starts playing (Distance_Dec_Past), where it stops (Distance_Inc_Past) and where its volume curve reaches silence — all read from that sound's own .sms.
  • Same sound in many places: the selected panel shows Used at N places with ◀ ▶ to slide through each place. Tick Group same sound in the list to see them together.
  • Filter by area: choose a station in Near station (sorted nearest first) and set the radius. Every sound always shows the nearest station names.
  • Remove: select and press Remove (or Delete). Removed sounds are shown crossed out until you save.

Hearing the route

Quick listen: select a sound and press ▶ Play — it plays the sound's own .wav straight away, at any distance.

Switch on Route sound and move around. By default you hear from the ground under the camera (a cyan ring marks the spot) — as if you stood there — so it also works from high up and in Map view. Choose Hear from the camera to measure distance from the camera itself, as Open Rails does. If nothing is in range the bottom bar tells you the nearest sound and how close you must get. Choose which sounds play: placed ssource.dat sounds, track sounds from ttype.dat (played at each sound region), or all. Choose how: exactly as the .sms files define, only the nearest one, or only the selected one.

"As the files define" follows each .sms: the sound wakes inside its Activation distance and sleeps beyond its Deactivation distance; a StartLoop stream starts when you come closer than its Distance_Dec_Past value and stops when you move further than its Distance_Inc_Past value; its loudness follows the DistanceControlled VolumeCurve and Volume; direction comes from where it stands (3D sound). Every sound bar in the list shows its live level, distance and state. Mute any sound with its speaker button.

Save, undo, exit

Save (Ctrl+S) writes all placed and removed sounds. Undo (Ctrl+Z) steps back through unsaved changes. Reload re-reads the route from disk. Exit editor asks to save if anything is unsaved. Unsaved changes are counted on the tab and next to the Save button.

What changes on disk

Placed sounds live in <route>\WORLD\w-XXXXXX+ZZZZZZ.ws (one file per 2 km tile), exactly as TSRE5 writes them: a Soundsource ( Position ( x y z ) FileName ( name.sms ) UiD ( n ) ) block, UTF-16 text. New sounds get the next free UiD of that tile (100000 and up). Track sound regions (Soundregion) and everything else in the file are left exactly as they were. Before the first change to a file it is copied to %LOCALAPPDATA%\StationAnnouncementMaker\world-backups\<route>\<date-time>. Restart TSRE5 / Open Rails to see changes there.

Graphics & speed

Before the window opens the app checks your graphics drivers: a dedicated GPU (NVIDIA / AMD Radeon RX / Intel Arc) with a driver is used for the 3D view; otherwise the built-in graphics chip; with no driver at all it falls back to software drawing (slower). You can force a choice in Settings → Graphics (applies on next start). Only the tiles near the camera are loaded, so even very large routes stay smooth.
